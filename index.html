Hello! I'm a first year PhD student in Computer Science at Harvard University, advised by Professor Kianté Brantley and funded by a Kempner Graduate Fellowship.

I have come from the beaver hills, amiskwaciy "Edmonton" (Plains Cree) , to the great hill, muhsachuweesut "Massachusetts" (Wampanoag) .

I am fascinated by language(s) and their acquisition by humans and machines. How does human language-learning differ from language-model training?

I am also intrigued by identity. What is there, if anything, that makes me who I am and not somebody else?
